Privacy Policy for Tumblin' Shapes

Effective date: August 27, 2026

Tumblin' Shapes ("the app") is developed by CausalCore ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the app does — and does not — collect, since the app is designed to be used by children.

Short version

Tumblin' Shapes does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, from children or adults. There is no account creation, no internet connection required to play, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party data collection of any kind. A few simple preferences (described below) are stored only on your own device and never leave it.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No name, email address, date of birth, or other identifying information is ever requested or collected.
  • No device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics are collected or transmitted.
  • No account or sign-in is required or offered.
  • No data is sent to us, or to any third party, at any time.

Information stored on your device

The app stores a small amount of data locally on your device, using standard iOS storage, purely to remember your own settings between visits:

  • Your selected language.
  • Whether sound is turned on or off.
  • Whether you've already seen the app's short first-launch introduction.

This information never leaves your device, is never transmitted to us or anyone else, and is automatically removed if you delete the app.

Device sensors

The app uses your device's motion sensor (tilt) so that shapes on screen respond as you tilt the device. This motion data is used only in real time, on your device, to control the game — it is never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

Third-party services

Tumblin' Shapes does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.

Children's privacy

Tumblin' Shapes is designed for use by children, including children under 13. Because we do not collect any personal information from any user — child or adult — the app complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar children's privacy laws by design: there is simply no data collection to disclose or consent to.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does collect information — we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
