TUMBLIN’ SHAPES

Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED — AUGUST 2026

Tumblin’ Shapes is made for young children, so it is built to collect nothing. The app runs entirely on your device and does not gather, store, or transmit personal information.

Information we collect

None. Tumblin’ Shapes does not ask for a name, email address, date of birth, or any account. It does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or location, and it does not read or write data outside its own app storage.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you or your child does in the app is recorded or reported to us or to anyone else.

Children’s privacy

Because no personal information is collected, there is nothing for a parent or guardian to review, correct, or delete. The app has no external links, no social features, no chat, no in-app purchases, and no way to communicate with other players.

Data stored on your device

Any local settings or progress stay on your device and are removed when the app is deleted. This information is never sent to us.

Apple App Store

Downloads and purchases are handled by Apple, not by CausalCore. Apple’s own privacy practices apply to the App Store itself and are described in Apple’s privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy are welcome at causalcorestudio@gmail.com.
